How It Works

Reading a tracking number

Every carrier uses its own format. Here is what the tool looks for.

UPS

Starts with 1Z followed by 16 characters. Example: 1Z999AA10123456784. UPS numbers are the easiest to spot.

USPS

Usually 20 to 22 digits, often starting with 94, 93, 92, or 95. International parcels use two letters, nine digits, two letters.

FedEx and DHL

FedEx uses 12, 15, or 20 digit numbers. DHL Express uses 10 or 11 digits. Amazon Logistics numbers start with TBA.

OnTrac and LaserShip

Common for last mile delivery. Formats include numbers starting with C or D, or with a 1LS prefix.

Unknown format

Some marketplaces use internal reference numbers. The tool then offers universal trackers that check every carrier at once.
