Typical Timelines

How long refunds take

Countdowns start when the store issues the refund, not when you request it. Always confirm the issue date first.

Cards: 5 to 10 business days

Credit and debit refunds pass through the card network and your bank. Pending line items that vanish and return are normal mid cycle.

Bank transfer: 3 to 5 business days

ACH refunds are quicker but only move on business days. A Friday refund often starts moving on Monday.

Wallets: 3 to 5 business days

PayPal and digital wallets credit fast, then withdrawing to your bank adds its own 1 to 3 day leg.

Gift cards: 1 to 3 business days

Store credit is the fastest since no bank is involved. Check spam for the new code email before worrying.

Common delays

Expired cards, closed accounts, mismatched names, and holiday backlogs stall refunds. Each has a fix, and each fix starts with proof of issue.
