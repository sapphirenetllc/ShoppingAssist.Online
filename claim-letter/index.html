What Works

Anatomy of a winning claim

Support teams resolve clear, documented claims first. Give them one.

One issue, one message

State exactly what went wrong in the first sentence. Agents skim, so the point must land before line three.

Facts attached

Order number, date, amount, and photos. A claim with evidence skips two rounds of back and forth questions.

A real deadline

Ask for resolution within 3 business days. Open ended requests sink to the bottom of every queue.

Calm leverage

Mention a payment dispute as a next step, not a threat. Polite firmness settles faster than anger, every time.

Keep copies

Save every message and reference number. If the store stalls, that paper trail is what wins the chargeback.

Need backup?

If the seller ignores you or the claim gets denied unfairly, call us and we plan the escalation with you.
