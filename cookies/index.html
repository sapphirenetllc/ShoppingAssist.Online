1. Cookies we set: none for tracking

ShoppingAssist does not use first-party tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. Our free tools keep what you type only in your browser's short-term page memory, which disappears when you close or refresh the page. We do not store your tracking numbers, refund dates, or claim drafts on our servers.

2. Third-party content

To keep the site fast and readable we load two widely used libraries:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) for the Inter typeface;
  • Font Awesome via cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) for icons.

Those providers may receive standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) under their own privacy policies when your browser fetches the files. They do not receive anything you type into our tools. We place no social-media pixels and no ad tags on this site.

3. How to manage cookies

Because we set no optional cookies, there is nothing to opt out of here. To control cookies generally:

  • use your browser's Settings > Privacy to block third-party cookies or clear site data;
  • use private / incognito mode if you want zero local history;
  • note that blocking all cookies does not break our tools, which do not depend on cookies.

If we ever add an optional feature that needs storage (such as remembering a preference), we will ask first and explain how to turn it off.
